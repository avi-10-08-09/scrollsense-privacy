SCROLLSENSE / ABHIANNU STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Android application · Effective November 18, 2026

Privacy at a glance. ScrollSense is designed to help you understand your own short-video usage. The public release is intended to process tracking information on your device, without accounts, advertising or remote tracking-data synchronization.

1. Scope

This policy describes how ScrollSense, published by Abhiannu Studio, handles information in its public Android release (package: app.scrollsense.tracker). It does not govern YouTube, Instagram, Android, Google Play or other independent services.

2. Information processed

To provide its dashboard and usage insights, ScrollSense processes the supported app or feed type, encounter counts, viewing durations, dates, session information and app preferences. It may process Android usage statistics, including app identifiers and usage durations, to display app usage information.

When enabled, the Accessibility Service examines interface elements of supported short-video feeds. For YouTube Shorts, available channel and caption information may be read temporarily and converted into a SHA-256 fingerprint to distinguish encounters. Instagram Reels tracking may use page-transition information and generated identifiers. Tracking identifiers are used to avoid duplicate counts and recognize transitions, not to identify the user. Fingerprinting is not encryption.

ScrollSense is designed not to retain raw video captions or author names as tracking history. It is not designed to record video, audio, screenshots, passwords, private messages or typed input. Counts are estimates and may be incomplete when a supported app does not expose necessary interface information.

3. Android permissions and your choices

  • Usage Access: allows the app to read Android app usage statistics for its usage dashboard.
  • Accessibility Service: detects YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, recognizes encounters and measures feed viewing time. It is intended for personal usage awareness, not as a disability assistance tool. It is optional and requires your affirmative consent and activation in Android settings.
  • Internet access: the app includes the Android internet permission. Development synchronization is disabled in the public release. The app does not intentionally send your tracking records to a remote server.

You can withdraw access by disabling ScrollSense in Android's Accessibility and Usage Access settings. Disabling these permissions limits the corresponding tracking features. Previously stored records remain until deleted.

4. Storage, use and sharing

Tracking records and preferences are stored in app-private storage on your device, including a local SQLite database. They are used to calculate counts, viewing time and history, display insights and operate the app. The public release is designed without a user account, advertising, paid features or third-party analytics services. Abhiannu Studio does not intentionally collect tracking records on its servers, sell them, or share them with third parties for advertising or profiling.

Android and external apps or services may independently process information under their own policies. If you email support, your email address, message and any information you voluntarily provide are received and used to respond to your request.

5. Security, backups and data retention

ScrollSense uses Android app-private storage and operating-system access controls. The release is configured to disable Android automatic app backup and exclude app data from supported cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. This does not guarantee deletion of earlier backups or prevent every operating-system or manufacturer-specific transfer mechanism. ScrollSense does not claim that its local database has additional application-level encryption.

Local records are retained while the app stores them on your device. To delete your locally stored ScrollSense records and preferences, open Android Settings, select Apps > ScrollSense > Storage, and choose Clear storage (or Clear data). Alternatively, uninstall the app. These actions remove local history and may reset permissions and settings. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to address inquiries and meet applicable obligations; you may request deletion by contacting us.

6. Age and intended audience

ScrollSense is intended for people aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13. Younger users should use the app with parental or guardian guidance as appropriate. Where local law requires parental consent, a parent or guardian should provide it before use.

7. Privacy of this website

This policy page does not include advertising, analytics scripts, forms or embedded third-party media. GitHub Pages hosts the page and may process technical information, such as IP addresses, according to its privacy statement.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if ScrollSense's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated. Material changes to permission use will be disclosed in the app as required.
